What is Builder Market?

Builder Market is two-sided marketplace that connects homeowners seeking home services with professionals who provide them. Homeowners can easily find and book trusted professionals for their home improvement needs, while service providers can expand their customer base and grow their businesses through the platform.

Despite attracting a lot of monthly visitors, the platform faced heavy user bounce rate during onboarding.

Identified Challenges

Through research and funnel analysis, we identified a set of challenges that contributed to early drop-off during onboarding.

1

No early value demonstration

Users were asked to invest significant time completing forms before experiencing any tangible benefit, creating a high-effort, low-reward perception that drove early drop-off.

Competitor Analysis
2

Unclear progress and time commitment

The absence of clear progress indicators made it difficult for users to gauge how long listing creation would take, creating uncertainty and making the process feel endless.

Heuristic Analysis
3

Poor visual hierarchy amplified perceived effort

Oversized input fields and dense form layouts exaggerated the perceived length of the process, discouraging users before they even began.
