Hello! I’m Sai (/sai/ -- rhymes with "hi")

I design end-to-end product solutions to be intuitive and delightful. Along the way, I like having a good time with the people I build them with.

I studied biotechnology & bioinformatics. Before UX design, I was an analyst in pharmaceutical research & development industry where I helped develop a tool called seewise to manage and visualise clinical trial data. And that was my window to UX & design.

Outside of work, I enjoy exploring new dishes, watching cooking videos, doodling comics drawing from my childhood, reading, and occasionally painting. I’m learning to grow into adulthood while keeping my inner child alive.

Current values

Some values that I’ve consciously built in me through years of learning through reflections & introspections.

Curiosity & strength

I ask the questions that have gone unasked for too long. This didn’t come easily; It required me to stop seeking safety in external spaces and instead build my mind to be a safe place for my thoughts.

Experiences & learning

I maintain an open mind to learn from diverse stories of past and present. I think stories are great, through them I’m invited to experiences & lessons which I could have never possibly imagined.

Intuitions & taste

Intuition is the outcome of reflection and introspection. Taste is a sacred quality of a person. These both qualities are strengths I actively cultivate.
